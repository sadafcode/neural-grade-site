01 — The Problem

A great product with no marketing playbook

Pakistani school teachers spend 2–3 days grading 200 papers every week. Neural Grade solves this — AI grades in seconds, teacher approves. But no local competitor existed, no playbook to follow.

The marketing challenge: reach the right people, build trust, and do it without a single false claim.

🗺️
No Local Competitor
First-mover advantage — but no reference point. Had to build strategy from first principles.
🇵🇰
Urdu-First Market
International tools fail Pakistani teachers. Messaging had to be hyper-local — Urdu/English mix.
🤝
Trust-Based Sale
"AI replaces teachers" fears are real. Messaging had to be "AI assists, teacher decides."
📱
WhatsApp-First Audience
Teachers live on WhatsApp & Facebook — not LinkedIn. Strategy had to reflect where they actually are.

03 — What I Built

A system, not just a plan

Marketing that works long-term needs infrastructure — not just a few posts. Here's what I designed:

01
🔍
Competitive Research & Market Gap
Mapped Gradescope, Turnitin, ZipGrade against Neural Grade. Found the clear gap: Urdu support + local pricing + any paper format — no configuration. No local competitor exists.
Deliverable: Competitive Matrix
02
🎯
Positioning & Messaging Framework
Defined a positioning statement, 5 unique selling points, and a messaging framework. Core message: "3 din ka kaam, 30 minutes mein." Backed by real product specs, not marketing fluff.
Deliverable: Positioning Doc
03
🧠
Truth-Based Claims System
Built a strict NEVER/ALWAYS claims guide. Every piece of marketing copy runs through it before publishing. No "100% accurate." No "instant grading." No false promises.
Deliverable: Claims Guide
04
📝
Full Content Engine
20+ copy-ready templates across every channel. WhatsApp cold outreach, Facebook posts, LinkedIn reply templates, email sequences, objection handlers — all in Urdu/English mix.
Deliverable: Templates Library
05
🌐
Landing Page Copy
Complete landing page copy — hero, pain points, how it works, feature comparison, FAQ, CTAs. Written in Urdu/English mix matching the target audience's daily language.
Deliverable: Landing Page Copy
06
📅
12-Week Execution Plan
52 granular tasks, phased across Beta Launch → Paid Launch → Scale. Each task has a clear owner, deliverable, and success metric. With budget allocation and risk mitigation built in.
Deliverable: Launch Strategy
07
🤖
Marketing Automation Skills
Built custom Claude Code skills for automated content generation. LinkedIn Image Generator reads post content, analyzes messaging, and generates AI prompts with brand colors. Outputs 3 prompt versions for different AI tools.
Deliverable: 7 Custom Skills
08
🎨
AI-Powered Visual Content
Integrated free AI image generation workflow using Gemini + Canva tool. Brand-consistent visuals (dark navy #060b18, indigo #6366f1, purple #8b5cf6) generated in minutes. No design skills required.
Deliverable: Image Generation System
09
📊
Live Marketing Dashboard
Built a fully deployed marketing command center (Netlify). Tracks beta testers, leads, LinkedIn/Facebook post statuses, auto follow-up flags, conversion rate, and weekly report generator — all in one place.
Deliverable: Live Dashboard
10
🔄
Google Sheets Two-Way Sync
Dashboard data syncs to Google Sheets in real time — works across mobile and laptop. Google Form responses automatically populate the Beta Testers sheet. Duplicate prevention via email check.
Deliverable: Multi-Device Data Sync
11
📋
Beta & Feedback Forms
Two live Google Forms: Beta Tester Signup (with email automation on submission) and a 16-field Review Form (screenshot upload, issue tracking, feature feedback). Both trigger automated email notifications and sheet sync.
Deliverable: Feedback Infrastructure

05 — Key Decisions

The choices that shaped everything

Every strategy is defined by what you choose not to do as much as what you do.

✋
Truth-Based Marketing Only
All claims verified against the actual product before publishing. A claims guide governs every piece of copy.
Never: "100% accurate" → Always: "AI suggests, teacher approves"
👩‍🏫
B2C First, B2B Later
Target individual teachers directly — not schools as institutions. Simpler sale, faster feedback loop.
Individual teacher adoption → then scale to schools
💬
WhatsApp as Primary Channel
Pakistani teachers live on WhatsApp — not email. The strategy goes where the audience actually is.
WhatsApp outreach → video dekho → message karo → onboarding
🆓
Free Beta, Then Paid
No payment pressure upfront. Get real usage data, build testimonials, then charge with confidence.
Free trial → feedback → Rs. 1,500/month
